Home page (not signed in): a better design

What's wrong with the page now, and two cleaner options. Both are shown at a real desktop size (scaled down) with the header and the footer, so you see the whole first screen.

1 What's wrong today

  1. The logo three times: in the header, on the picture panel, and in the footer.

Everything sits in the same column as the header and footer. The drawing is shown whole in a rounded frame, with two small floating cards that say what the portal does. One title, one line, two buttons, three short points. The slim footer is visible on the first screen.
