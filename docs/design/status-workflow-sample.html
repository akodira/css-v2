Ticket status workflow: suggested

The same eight staff statuses as today. Customers see five simple names. Four gaps closed. Anything marked NEW does not exist today.

1 The whole flow on one page

Boxes are the staff names; the small line under each is what the customer sees. Orange lines are the new moves.

2 What the customer sees

Staff keep all eight statuses. Customers see five names, each with one plain sentence on the ticket page. Only New and Open change: both become "Received", because the difference (assigned or not) means nothing to the customer.

3 The four fixes

1 A customer reply on a Resolved ticket reopens it

Today

The customer writes "still not working". The ticket stays Resolved and still closes itself on day 5. Nobody is told.

Suggested

The reply moves the ticket to Reopened, stops the day-5 timer and tells the assigned agent.

CTS-2026-000062Reopened
Thanks. Your reply has reopened the ticket and we will look at it again.

2 Customers can cancel their own ticket while it is "Received"

Today

No way to cancel. The customer has to write "please close it" and an agent closes it.

Suggested

A Cancel ticket button on the ticket page while it is New or Open. It asks to confirm, then closes with the reason "Customer cancellation". The customer can reopen it within 30 days.

CTS-2026-000071Received
Opened today · not started yetCancel ticket

3 Staff can close from any active status, with a reason

Today

Staff can close only from New or Waiting for you. A duplicate found while In progress must be marked Resolved first, and the customer gets a "resolved" email that is not true.

Suggested

"Closed" is in the status list for every active status. It needs a closure reason and a note. Still needs the "Close tickets" permission.

4 Pending internal gets a reminder

Today

No timer. A ticket can wait on another team forever while the customer sees "In progress".

Suggested

After 3 days in Pending internal, the assigned agent and their team leader get an email. It repeats every 3 days. It never closes by itself. The number of days is set with the other ticket timers in Settings (0 = off).
