Ticket status flow in the Help Center

The workflow drawn as a real diagram in Help Center → Tickets → Flows. There are two versions, one for customers and one for staff, and each person sees only their own. It shows the system as it works today. If we build the status fixes later, the diagram is updated with them.

Correction to my last sample: two of the "fixes" already exist. Customers can already cancel a ticket. If no one has replied yet it closes at once; otherwise staff approve or reject the request. Staff can already close from any status with the Duplicate and Spam buttons. Only two real gaps remain: a customer reply on a Resolved ticket, and no reminder for Pending internal.

1 What customers see

This replaces today's flat "The journey of your ticket" chain. It uses the customer names, and the days come from Settings, so they are always right. It is drawn with page boxes (not a picture), so it mirrors in Arabic and stacks on a phone.

Cancel ticket at any time before it is resolved. If no one has replied yet it closes at once; otherwise the team reviews your request.
Waiting for youWe remind you on days 2 and 4 and warn you on day 7. With no reply, it closes on day 10.
ResolvedWe remind you on day 3. If you do not confirm, it closes by itself on day 5.
ClosedYou can reopen it within 30 days. After that, start a follow-up ticket.

3 How it is built

CustomersMade of page boxes, so it mirrors in Arabic, stacks on a phone, and screen readers read it as a list in order. The day numbers come from the ticket timers in Settings.
StaffA drawing with every label in English and Arabic. The Arabic version mirrors the layout. On a phone it scrolls sideways inside its card, and the page itself never scrolls sideways.
