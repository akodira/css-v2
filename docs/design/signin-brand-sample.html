Sign-in pages: the logo block

Today the logo, name and tagline sit in a white box on top of the picture. Three ways to fix it, each shown at a real desktop size (scaled down). The same change applies to every sign-in page: sign in, code, two-step, create account, forgot and new password.

1 What's wrong today

  1. It covers the drawing: the speech-bubble icon in English and the check mark in Arabic sit right under the box.
  2. It looks pasted on: a see-through white box floating on a blue picture, with no line to anything else on the page.
  3. Two brands compete: the box at the top and "We're here to help" at the bottom both shout.
  4. The picture is cut: it fills the panel by cropping, so the person and the desk change size and position with the window.

2 Option A: logo above the sign-in card Recommended

The logo, a thin line, and the name and tagline sit centred above the card, so people see them right where they sign in. The picture has only the drawing (shown whole, never cut) and the band. The phone strip goes away because the same block shows above the card on every screen. It matches the home page, where the logo also sits away from the picture.

3 Option B: a white strip across the top of the picture

The brand stays on the picture side, but in its own white strip from edge to edge, with the logo, a line, and the name and tagline in one row. The drawing starts below the strip, so nothing is covered. It is tidy, but the brand sits far from the form.

4 Option C: logo on the picture, without the box

The logo, name and tagline sit straight on the light top of the picture, with no box. The drawing is moved down and made smaller so the top stays clear. It is the closest to today, but it only works while the top of the picture is light. An uploaded dark picture would hide the logo.
